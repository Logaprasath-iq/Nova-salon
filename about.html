Our Story

A Decade of Luxury Craftsmanship

NOVA was founded in 2014 with a simple, disruptive vision: beauty services shouldn't be generic. They should represent custom-designed art combined with scientific skin and hair care health.

Starting as a small styling studio in the luxury district, NOVA quickly expanded. Over the years, we integrated autoclave medical sanitization, dermal scope diagnostics, and frequency sound bath therapy chambers. Today, we stand as the primary luxury wellness sanctuary for discerning guests.

Sophia Laurent

Founder & Creative Director

Our Mission

To provide a serene, safe, and hygienic sanctuary where clients experience bespoke luxury treatments that elevate their organic aesthetic beauty and mental wellness.

Our Vision

To lead the luxury styling and wellness space by introducing scientifically certified, eco-friendly dermal therapies and setting global sanitation standards.
